Help & Support

MysticVeil · Tarot readings by Jalpa

Need help? Email Jalpa at jalpasp@gmail.com. You’ll usually hear back within 48 hours.

How does a reading work?

Write your question, spin the wheel and draw four cards (Past, Present, Future and Outcome), then enter your name and email. Jalpa personally interprets your spread and emails your reading, usually within 24–48 hours.

I haven’t received my reading

Please check your spam or promotions folder first. If it’s been more than 48 hours, email Jalpa with the name and email you used.

My request didn’t send

Check your internet connection and tap “Try Again”. If it still fails, use the “email it to Jalpa directly” link that appears, which fills in your question and cards for you.

What does “Reversed” mean?

A reversed card was drawn upside down. In tarot it often softens, delays or turns inward the card’s usual meaning; Jalpa covers this in your reading.

Is tarot advice?

Readings are offered for reflection, insight and entertainment. They are not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.
