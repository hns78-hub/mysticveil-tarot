Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

MysticVeil offers personal tarot readings by Jalpa through this website and the MysticVeil app. This policy explains what information we collect and how it is used.

What we collect

We only collect what you enter when you request a reading:

  • Your name and email address, so Jalpa can send your reading;
  • Your question, the four cards you drew, and any additional context you choose to share.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking, and we do not create accounts.

How it is used

Your request is used only to prepare and email your reading, and to reply if you write back. We never sell or share your information for marketing.

Who processes it

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers your request to Jalpa by email.
  • Email providers (Jalpa’s and yours) store the messages exchanged.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and Google Fonts serves its fonts; like any web host, they may receive your IP address when pages load.

Retention and your choices

Reading requests are kept in Jalpa’s email for as long as needed to provide and follow up on your reading. You can ask for your information to be deleted at any time by emailing jalpasp@gmail.com.

Children

MysticVeil is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: jalpasp@gmail.com
